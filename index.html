05 / Why PCI

Two lenses.Different questions.

JATO tells you what every car is. PCI tells you what the market charges for it today, and whether buyers can afford it.

JATOThe reference catalogue
PCIThe live market signal
The question
What does every car offer, and at what list price?
What is each brand charging buyers this month, and can they afford it?
Source
Standardised industry database, licensed through its platform and API.
Read from 443 official brand websites: configurators, catalogues, dealer stock.
Breadth
58 markets · 200+ brands · 1,000+ attributes per vehicle. The specification reference.
17 markets · 66 brands, built around Astara’s footprint, incl. Bolivia and Chinese importers.
Price per vehicle
Catalogue prices per version: list price, options, packs and colours.
Six price layers per version, plus real cars in dealer stock: 15,800 units with equipment, colour and asking price.
Affordability
Not part of its published specification offer.
Price against average income in each country: weeks of gross salary per car, adjusted to purchasing power (Eurostat PPS).
Market share
Registration volumes, offered as a separate module.
Share by brand and model, next to price and affordability in one view.
Control
A standard product and taxonomy, shared by the whole industry.
Owned by Astara. New brands, markets or fields added on demand, in weeks.

Complementary, not a replacement. JATO is the reference; PCI is the signal Astara owns.

JATO: jato.com (specifications coverage; press release, 10 Aug 2026). PCI: Full Run, October 2026; affordability from Eurostat price levels and average salaries; share from 2026 YTD registrations.
